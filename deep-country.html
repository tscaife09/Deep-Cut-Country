How to Play

  • Type one valid answer. Spelling is flexible.
  • Rarer answers score higher: Boot Scootin’ (10) → One in a Million (100).
  • Every point = 10 miles down the backroads. 7000 miles is the end of the highway.
  • Too Clever = the “obscure” answer that everyone actually thinks of.
